```text
Hi {first_name},

Delighted you are joining us on {project_or_context}. Here is where everything
happens.

SLACK

- {slack_connect_link} gets you into our workspace through Slack Connect.
- #jinesis-with-friends-and-collaborators — our wider circle.
- #jinesis-active and #random-active — the lab's day-to-day, work and otherwise.
- {discussion_channel} — the broader topic your work sits in.

GOOGLE DRIVE

Your project folder: {drive_folder_link}

A few conventions that save a lot of friction later: one long doc per topic rather
than several tabs, kept Pageless, filenames prefixed with the date (yyyymmdd), and a
flat folder so sorting by last-modified stays useful.

HOW WE WORK

Docs first, then Slack, then a short call. Pass by reference in Slack and keep the
detail in your doc. Send a doc link the day before a meeting with your progress and
what you want to discuss.

I will send two short notes separately — our Google file practices and what to
expect working with us. Both are quick and worth reading before you get going.

If you would like to follow the lab more widely: our newsletter,
https://www.linkedin.com/company/jinesis-lab/, and https://x.com/JinesisLab. And you
are welcome at our city dinners and team building events.

Best,
{sender_name}
```
