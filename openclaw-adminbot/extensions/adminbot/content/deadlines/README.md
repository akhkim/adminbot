# AdminBot — Deadline Tracker

Lab-wide conference & workshop **deadline collection + reminders** for
`#jinesis-active`. All times are **AoE (UTC-12)**.

## Contents

| File                   | What it is                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `venues.json`          | Canonical tracked deadlines (Output 0/1/2 all read this). Regenerated by `scripts/adminbot-deadline-collect.py`. |
| `dm-templates.json`    | Venue-aware DM reminder templates (30/15/7/3/2/1 steps + escalation + "stop" ack).                               |
| `matches.json`         | Papers → deadlines, produced by `scripts/adminbot-deadline-match.py` (git-ignored / regenerated).                |
| `deadlines-board.html` | **Output 0** — self-contained live countdown board (served page / Control-UI tab source).                        |

## Scripts (`../../../../scripts/`)

- `adminbot-deadline-collect.py` — refresh `venues.json` from official CFPs and the eight requested OpenReview workshop families.
- `adminbot-deadline-match.py` — map **ongoing** papers (`Venue` column) and **ready** papers (topic-match → collected workshops) to deadlines → `matches.json`.
- `adminbot-deadline-reminders.py` — **Output 2** runner: fire the due 30/15/7/3/2/1-day author DMs, escalate to Zhijing past deadline. **Dry-run by default** (`--send` to deliver).

## The venue set (from Zhijing's guidebook)

ACL / EMNLP / NAACL / EACL (main + demo), NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR / COLM / CLeaR
(main), IASEAI (non-archival), and the **workshops** of the eight — CLeaR is
tracked for its main track only. AACL main/demo are tracked as secondary archival;
AACL workshops are intentionally outside the sweep.

### Independent venue classifications

Every row stores three separate facts:

- `entry_type` says whether it is a main conference, demo track, workshop, ARR
  direct submission, ARR commitment, rebuttal, or other date.
- `archival_status` is `archival`, `non_archival`, `mixed`, or `unknown`. Unknown remains
  unknown; absence from an archival list is never presented as evidence that a
  submission is safe.
- `venue_priority` is the lab's `primary`, `secondary`, or `standard` priority.

ACL, EMNLP, and NAACL main/demo plus NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, COLM, and CLeaR are
primary archival. EACL and AACL main/demo are secondary archival. IASEAI is the
only explicitly non-archival family. NeurIPS workshop papers are non-archival
under the official 2026 workshop guidance. Other workshops follow their own CFP:
`mixed` means it explicitly offers both routes, and `unknown` is reserved for a
page with no applicable publication policy.

The guidebook's non-archival workshop bucket is a lab-planning category, not a
claim that every workshop route is non-archival. The stored status describes the
actual route(s) offered; when both exist, the lab should choose the non-archival
route if it intends to submit the paper elsewhere.

The ARR-family, archival-priority, and workshop-sweep sets are declared
independently in `scripts/adminbot_deadlines.py`. This keeps AACL in ARR and the
secondary archival set without silently adding AACL workshops to the sweep.

### ARR routes

The \*ACL venues take papers two ways, and which one is open depends on the
paper's history. Each is its own dated entry, tagged `submission_type`:

- `direct` — submit fresh into the ARR cycle.
- `commitment` — attach reviews the paper already has to a specific venue.

OpenReview names commitment venues with an `_ARR_Commitment` suffix, which is
what the collector classifies on.

### Sub-deadlines

A venue is not one date. A conference runs an abstract deadline, then the full paper, then
rebuttal, then camera-ready; an ARR venue runs a direct submission and a commitment. Each is
**its own row sharing a `venue_group`**, tagged with `milestone` (`abstract`,
`direct_submission`, `full_paper`, `demo`, `commitment`, `rebuttal`, `notification`,
`camera_ready`).
That is what lets the board group them under one conference and count down to each separately,
and what orders them in the sequence a paper meets them rather than by date alone.

Adding one is adding a row to the curated table with the right `deadline_label` —
`milestone_of()` in `scripts/adminbot_deadlines.py` maps the label onto the closed set. A row
whose label is not recognised still renders; it just sorts last and shows its raw label.

**OpenReview cannot supply these.** It exposes one dated stage per workshop (`-/Submission`) and
none at all for main conferences — abstract, camera-ready and rebuttal dates are simply not in
its group or invitation data. So sub-deadlines beyond the submission are curated, the same as
main-conference dates. ICLR 2027 carries abstract + full paper, EACL 2027 carries its demo track
alongside its ARR commitment, and NAACL 2027 carries both ARR routes.

### What the weekly sweep fetches

`refresh-venues` walks every tracked family's OpenReview workshop parent across a
rolling two-year window (never a pinned year — a hardcoded one would keep working
until its round closed and then return nothing forever without failing). Each
workshop's deadline comes from its **Submission invitation**, not its group: the
group's `date`/`start_date` are when the workshop _runs_, months after the CFP
closes. When an invitation is unavailable, the collector retains the last observed
deadline without pretending that it was rechecked.

Main-conference dates stay in the curated table in
`scripts/adminbot-deadline-collect.py` and always win — they change slowly and
must be exact. Every date there is off the venue's own CFP page and names it in a
comment. A tracked venue whose next round has not announced a date sits in
`PENDING` in the same file rather than getting a guessed one, and the collector
prints that list at the end of every run.

Each workshop stores its `homepage_url`, dedicated `cfp_url`, and `openreview_url`
separately. `cfp_url` stays empty when no dedicated CFP is found; the views then
label the homepage as “Workshop site” rather than misrepresenting it as a CFP.
`source_url` identifies the OpenReview venue record and `source_checked_at` records
the UTC day when the deadline source was actually observed. The public views show
the CFP or homepage and OpenReview as explicit, separate actions.

The collector also reads visible CFP text for explicit publication policy. It
does not infer archival status from a parent conference, a workshop family, or
the absence of a statement. If a source fetch fails, the last established value
is retained together with the prior source timestamp.

## OpenReview stop-condition

Reminders stop once a paper shows in Zhijing's OpenReview submissions. That
needs an authenticated OpenReview session (blind in-review papers are invisible
otherwise), so **Zhijing enters `OPENREVIEW_USERNAME` / `OPENREVIEW_PASSWORD`
into the service secret store herself** — never committed here. Until then the
runner falls back to: run the full cadence, let authors reply **"done"** to stop,
and escalate to Zhijing at the deadline.

See `docs/tools/adminbot-deadlines.md` for setup, cron scheduling, delivery-mode
choice, and how to expose the board as a Control-UI tab.
